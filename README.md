## Project Overview
End-to-end retail analytics project analysing 10,000+ transactions 
to identify profitable products, loss making regions and the impact 
of discounts on business performance using Python, SQL and Tableau.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python** (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn) — Data cleaning & EDA
- **SQL** (SQLite) — Business queries & analysis
- **Tableau** — Interactive dashboard
- **Google Colab** — Development environment

## Project Structure
- `notebooks/01_data_cleaning.ipynb` — Data cleaning & feature engineering
- `notebooks/02_eda.ipynb` — Exploratory data analysis & charts
- `notebooks/03_sql_analysis.ipynb` — SQL business queries
- `notebooks/04_profit_segmentation.ipynb` — Product segmentation
- `superstore_queries.sql` — All SQL queries

## Key Insights
- West region generates highest revenue but Central has lowest profit
- Tables and Bookcases are the biggest loss making sub-categories
- High discounts above 20% consistently lead to losses
- Technology category has highest profit margin
- Consumer segment contributes most to overall revenue

## Dashboard
3-page interactive Tableau dashboard covering:
- Sales Overview (Revenue, Profit, Orders KPI cards + monthly trend)
- Product Analysis (Sub-category profit/loss, discount vs profit)
- Product Segments (Star products, Loss makers breakdown)

Sample Superstore Sales Dataset — available on Kaggle
